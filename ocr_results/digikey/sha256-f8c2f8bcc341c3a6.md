 Measurement principle     Thin film strain gauge (full Wheatstone bridge)

 Measurement parameter   Force [N]

 Measurement ranges      0 – 18 N to 0 – 300 N, see order information. Custom force ranges available on
                           request

                            Color scheme for stranded wire (-K1 variant):  Red:        Supply voltage + (VCC)
                                                                              Black:       Supply voltage – (GND)
                                                                       White:       Signal voltage +/-
                                                                           Blue:        Signal voltage -/+
 Mounting                Designed for installation in Ø12 mm blind hole, see prior section

 Application of force      On round-head rivet, sensor to be positioned in centrally in blind hole

 Direction of force          Perpendicular to sensor plane (see ordering information for variants)

 Shielding                 Sensor substrate is connected to supply ground

 Substrate material          Stainless Steel 1.4542 / X5CrNiCuNb16-4 / 17-4 PH®
