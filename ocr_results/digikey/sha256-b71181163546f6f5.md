FlexiForce™Standard Model A201



                                  The FlexiForce A201 is a piezoresistive force sensor engineered for measuring
                                         forces between two mating surfaces with ease. Its ultra-thin, flexible design
                                and multiple length options make it an ideal choice for applications requiring
                                           non-intrusive, reliable force measurement. Whether you’re testing, prototyping,
                                     conducting research, or exploring proof-of-concept designs, the A201 delivers
                                         consistent results in tight spaces. Compatible with Tekscan electronics- including
                                       the FlexiForce Sensor Characterization Kit and the ELF System*- or your own
                                         setups, including multimeters, this sensor adapts to your needs with ease.
