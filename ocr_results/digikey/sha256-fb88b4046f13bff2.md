     APF-UF Series
            Ultra-thin Force Sensor





          Description
             Angst+Pfister’s newest technology is an ultra-thin force sensor with a thickness of
          under 1mm. This compact design allows integration into even the most space-
            constrained applications. With the encasing of a durable elastomer, the sensor is fully
            protected from moisture and environmental influences, ensuring reliable performance
              in diverse conditions.

          The sensor measures applied force under compression and is suitable for a wide range
             of applications.
           Geometry, mounting style, connector type, force range, and digital interface can be
           customized to project requirements.

          Application Example – Thin Kitchen Scale
         A statically defined three-point configuration ensures even load distribution and stable
           measurements. It is suitable for applications such as liquid tank weighing, kitchen scales
            or general load sensing. Additionally, the three sensor signals enable calculation of the
           weight distribution and the system’s centre of mass.

          Additional Information
