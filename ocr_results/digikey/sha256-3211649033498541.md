Testing setup:

The PNC force sensor was tested using a Universal Testing Machine (UTM), model
"Zwicki Z2.5TN" from Zwick-Roell. The UTM is equipped with two load cells: one for
measurements from 0.01 N to 5 N and the other for measurements from 2 N to 1000
N. Both load cells were utilized to characterize the full measurement range of the
sensor.





Notes:

   •  The sensor was securely mounted in the UTM using a plastic disc to ensure
      proper placement during measurements.
   •  A 0.2 mm thick 20mm metal disc was adhered to the underside of the sensor’s
      electrode to ensure flatness during testing.
   •  A 20 cm extension wire was used to connect the sensor to the electronics,
       resulting in an offset of approximately 600 sensor counts.
