# SB2 beam load cell



## product description

Heavy duty shear beam load cell range designed specifically for applications found in steel processing systems. The SB2 design is particularly robust and is the choice for high capacity ladle weighing systems, coil weighers and platform scales. Hermetic sealing and resistant paint coating permits the SB2 to used in the toughest of environments.

## applications

High capacity industrial platform scales, ladle weighing systems and coil weighing systems.

## key features

Tool steel construction

Hermetically sealed to IP68

Painted finish

High capacity beam up to 45t

Unique blind loading hole

Calibration in mV/V/Ω

## approvals

OIML approval to C1 (Y = 5,000) and C3 (Y = 15,000)

FM hazardous area approval

## accessories

Compatible range of hardware

Compatible range of electronics

## wiring

The load cell is provided with a shielded, 4 conductor cable (AWG 20).

Cable jacket polyurethane

Cable length: 9.1 m for 45 klb, 10.7 m for 100 klb

Cable diameter: 7.6 mm

The shield is floating

(On request the shield can be connected to the load cell body)
