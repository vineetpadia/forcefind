# IP66 MOUNTING ENCLOSURE FOR XT1000 INDICATOR AND HIGH SPEED TRANSMITTER



Image above shows XT1000-MOUNTING-BOX housing XT1000 transmitter and optional power supply. The XT1000 transmitter and power supply are provided separately.

The hermetically sealed polycarbonate XT1000-MOUNTING-BOX is an ideal solution for housing the XT1000 Weighing Indicator and High Speed Transmitter if it needs to be protected in an outdoor or industrial application. It offers IP66 ingress protection and a high degree of impact resistance. The XT1000-2000-PS power supply (if required) can also be housed inside this enclosure. A DIN rail and a pre-assembled earth terminal are fitted inside (as shown above) for attachment of the XT1000 unit and the AC power supply. As standard, mounting holes are provided for mounting the box to a wall or bulkhead.

6 off plastic cable glands are provided for connecting of the load cell, power and communications cables.

- Impact-resistant (IK 07) polycarbonate construction
- Ideal for industrial and outdoor applications
- Complete with 6 off plastic cable glands (4 off PG7 and 2 off PG9)
- Protection class IP66
- Includes pre-assembled standard 35mm DIN rail and earth terminal
- Wall mountable
