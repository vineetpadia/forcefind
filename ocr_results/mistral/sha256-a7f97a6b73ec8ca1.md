# H8Q

中航讯网

ZEMIC

Measuring Excellence Worldwide



- H8Q load cells are available in the capacities 200kg~2t.
- Alloy steel, sealed by adhesive inside, oil-proof, waterproof and anti-corrosion, suitable for all kinds of environments.
- Single shear beam, easy for installation, suitable for electronic platform, hopper scales, floor scales and other electronic weighing devices.
