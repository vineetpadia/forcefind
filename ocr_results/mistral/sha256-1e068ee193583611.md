Model 5312

# Draft/Load Pin

FEATURES

- Load ranges: 200–500 kN
- Rugged design with zinc-plated, hardened alloy steel construction
- Ratio metric voltage output converter embedded

APPLICATIONS

- Agriculture equipment
- Force measurement devices
- Off-highway vehicles

DESCRIPTION

The Model 5312 load pin is designed to provide force measurement on an applied load across it. Internally bonded foil-based strain gages, bonded onto a robust zinc-plated, Case hardened alloy steel body, making it an ideal sensor for any demanding environment application.

This compact, rugged 50.5 mm load pin is available in three capacities up to 500 kN and, with a safe overload capability of up to 700 kN, delivers excellent long term stability and reliable operation, even under severe load



conditions. These features make the Model 5312 able to perform repeatable measurements in any given control or safety system.

The Model 5312 load pin is an ideal partner for a wide variety of applications, such as those found in tractor hitch systems, plowing vehicles, cranes or tensioning systems that employ rope, chain or brake anchors. This load pin may be used in lieu of hydraulic pressure or draft pin sensors.
