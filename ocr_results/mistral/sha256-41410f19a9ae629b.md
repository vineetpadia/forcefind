# H6F

中软机网 ZEMIC
Measuring Excellence Worldwide





- H6F load cells are available in the capacities 50kg~2000kg.
- Alloy steel construction, potted by adhesive inside, oil proof, waterproof, anti-corrosive gas and medium making it suitable for all kinds of environments.
- Single point with parallel beam, suitable for electronic scales, platform scales, belt scales and other electronic weighing devices.
- Max. platform size:
  50kg~200kg 400×400mm
  250kg~500kg 600×800mm
  750kg~2000kg 1200×1200mm

# Features

- Capacity: 50kg~2000kg
- High accuracy
- Alloy steel construction
- Low profile and easy installation
