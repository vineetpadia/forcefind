# High Capacity Off-Center Single-Point Load Cell

FEATURES

- Capacities: 750, 1000, and 2000 kg
- Fully sealed for water resistance
- Side mount construction
- Anodized aluminum alloy
- OIML C3 approval
- Platform size: 48" x 48"/120 cm x 120 cm
- Optional
  - FM approval available

APPLICATIONS

- Platform scales (single load cell)
- Packaging machines
- Dosing/filling
- Belt scales/conveyor scales



DESCRIPTION

The Model HOC is a single-point load cell of side mount construction designed for platform scales, and hanging scales. It is a cost-effective load cell for scales of simple construction.

The HOC is constructed of anodized aluminum, and is environmentally sealed up to IP66, providing excellent protection against moisture and humidity.
