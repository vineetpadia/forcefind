# SINGLE POINT LOAD CELL

capacities 50kg - 400kg



The T12A double bending beam single point load cell is ideal for high accuracy weight measurement with off-centre loads when the environment does not require stainless steel. Its nickel plated alloy steel construction, with silicone sealing to IP66, provides a cost-effective solution that is approved to 4000 divisions OIML R60 Class C. As standard, the T12A is supplied with a screened polyurethane cable; this provides a higher level of resistance to cuts and damage than a PVC cable.

Typical applications include weighing platforms with dimensions up to 800mm x 800mm; bagging, filling and dosing machines; medical scales; checkweighers; small bucket and hopper systems.

- Nickel plated alloy steel load sensor
- Silicone sealed to IP66
- OIML R60 Class C 4000 divisions (C4)
- Cost-effective solution
- 5 year warranty
- High accuracy with off-centre loads
- 600x600 mm or 800x800 mm platform size (with load cell placed centrally under the platform)
- High durability Polyurethane cable provides higher resistance to chemicals than PVC
