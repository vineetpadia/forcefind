# Single Point Load Cell BEF-F with a Rated Load of 150 kg



Performance Features

- Single point load cell made of anodized aluminum
- IP67 protection level
- Suited for tank weighing with side mounting
- Designed for platforms of maximum dimensions 600 mm x 600 mm (off-center load compensate)
- 6-wire technology
- Designed for use in harsh environments

Application

- Food industry
- Coal and steel industry
- Chemical / pharmaceutical industry
- Waste management / recycling
- Packaging technology
