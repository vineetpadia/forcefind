# SHEAR BEAM LOAD CELL FOR PLATFORM SCALES IN HARSH ENVIRONMENTS

capacities 300kg - 2000kg



Load foot is optional and ordered separately.

The T85-T stainless steel shear beam load cell is fully welded and hermetically sealed to IP68/IP69K and has been designed for use in high accuracy multi-cell platform scales in the harshest industrial environments. Calibration in mV/V/Ω (by output current matching) minimises the need for time consuming corner correction. The T85-T is approved to OIML R60 Class C3, with a blind loading hole and a durable polyurethane cable. Polyurethane cables provide greater resistance than PVC cables to chemicals, UV radiation, temperature changes and impacts.

For high accuracy platform scale applications, the T85-T can be used with the stainless steel load foot T85-T-FOOT-35916 (ordered separately).

As an option, T85-T load cells are available with dual-labelled ATEX and IECEx certification for all Gas and Dust zones. The certification for ATEX and IECEx dust zones 20, 21 and 22, and IECEx gas zone 2, does not require safety barriers, which creates a large cost saving.

For applications in aggressive environment where stress corrosion or acid attack is an issue, a special Parylene coating can be specified as an option to provide greater protection of the load cell.

For high temperature applications, an optional version of the T85-T is available for operation in environments up to 150°C (continuous temperature).

- Stainless steel construction
- 3000 divisions (Class C3) OIML R60 approval
- Calibrated in mV/V/Ω
- 5 year warranty
- High quality stainless steel cable entry
- Industry standard dimensions and specifications
- Option of 5 and 10 approval or Parylene coating or high temperature version
- Wide operating temperature range
