# Multi-Component Sensor M-2025 with Rated Force / Rated Torque
from 10 kN/10 N·m ... 20 kN/20 Nm





Data sheet

Performance Features

- Reaction torque/force sensor, non-rotating, for e.g. survey of the characteristics of elastomers
- TEDS (Transducer Electronic Data Sheet) Standard IEEE 1451.4 (optional)
- Very short axial length
- Reliable and durable
- Simple handling and assembly
- Special versions on request

Application

- Assembly technology
- Process measuring and control technology
- Automotive industry
- Measuring and control devices
- Tool engineering
- Special mechanical engineering
