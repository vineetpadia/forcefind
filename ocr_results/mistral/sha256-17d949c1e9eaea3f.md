## Double-Ended Shear Beam Load Cell

### FEATURES

- Capacities: 50k to 100k lbs
- Stainless steel construction

### APPLICATIONS

- Hopper/silo/tank weighing
- Weighbridges

### DESCRIPTION

The Model 9903 is a double-ended beam-type load cell with a low profile design, specifically designed for applications where space is limited or special mounting required.
