# B9F

中航机械 ZEMIC
Measuring Excellence Worldwide



- B9F load cells are available in the capacities 10Klb~75Klb.
- Stainless steel construction, potted by adhesive inside, oil proof, waterproof, anti-corrosive gas and medium making it suitable for all kinds of environments.
- Dual shear beam, suitable for electronic truck scales, hopper scales, track scales, and other electronic weighing devices.

# Features

- Capacity 10Klb~75Klb
- High accuracy
- Stainless steel construction,
- Easy installation and reliable performance
