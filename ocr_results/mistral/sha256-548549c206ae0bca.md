## Double-Ended Beam Load Cell

### FEATURES

- Capacities: 5k to 150k lbs
- Low profile construction
- Stainless steel construction
- Certified to NTEP class IIIL, 10000 divisions
- Sealing: IP67 (DIN 40.050)
- **Optional**
  - FM and ATEX certified versions are available for use in potentially explosive atmospheres
  - EDOC option available; product appearance will differ from the photograph due to coating



### APPLICATIONS

- Platform scales
- On-board weighing
- Weighbridges
- Silo hopper weighing

### DESCRIPTION

The Model 9103 is a double-ended, center-loaded shear beam type load cell constructed of stainless steel.

This product is suitable for tank weighing systems, low cost weighbridges and axle weighers.

A reliable sealing is ensured by the proprietary TRANSEAL potting compound and additional mechanical protection of the strain gage area.

A specially designed mounting arrangement is available, providing the ideal solution for vessel/tank weighing.
