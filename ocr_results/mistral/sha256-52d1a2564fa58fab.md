# Model 3132

## Tension/Compression Pancake Load Cell



DESCRIPTION

These general purpose designs have exceptional structural capability to withstand extraneous loads, such as torque, bending moments, and side loads.

Available in capacities of 500 lb to 5000 lb, they offer maximum performance and enhanced accuracy.

Minimum deflection and compactness make these general-purpose load cells easy to install and use.

FEATURES

- 500 lb to 5000 lb range
- Low sensitivity to extraneous loads
- Low deflection
- Rugged welded construction
- Built-in temperature compensation
