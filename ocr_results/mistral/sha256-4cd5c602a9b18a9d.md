# Bending Beam Load Cell F60X-6W with a Rated Load of 10 ... 500 kg



# Performance Features

- Bending beam load cell made of stainless steel, hermetically welded with protection class IP68
- Up to 6000d d OIML R60 and 5000d NTEP approved
- 6-wire technology
- Ideally suited for any industrial weighing application where a high accuracy is needed
- Signal output with an increased accuracy of ±0,05 % from rated output (option)
- ATEX 2014/34/EU version for hazardous area (option)

# Application

- Hoppers, tanks, mixers or conveyors
- Beverages and food
- Mining, metals and minerals
- Chemistry/Pharmaceuticals
- Waste management/recycling
- Packaging technology
