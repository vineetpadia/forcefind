# L6T

中航机械

ZEMIC

Measuring Excellence Worldwide



RoHS

- L6T load cells are available in the capacities 50kg~1000kg.
- Aluminum construction with parallel beam.
- Silicon adhesive sealed, surface anodized and anti-corrosion.
- Integral structure and easy installation.
- Suitable for electronic pricing scales, electronic platform scales and other electronic weighing devices.

# Features

- Capacity: 50kg~1000kg
- High accuracy
- Max. platform size: 600×600mm
