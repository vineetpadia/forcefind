# XT50 CAN extensometer



## product description

The XT50 CAN is a fully welded, stainless steel bolt-on extensometer, designed for load and stress monitoring in structural parts, overload sensing, and stability control on mobile and stationary construction and agricultural equipment.

Ideal for stiff structures, the XT50 CAN features low activation force and high-resolution output proportional to elastic deformation. Its bolt-on design ensures straightforward installation without adhesives.

An embedded CAN board transforms the XT50 into a fully digital extensometer, enabling direct CAN communication without requiring external signal converters. This ensures seamless integration into modern monitoring and control systems.

Setup is straightforward and can be performed with a terminal emulation program or the Flintec FDC application, available from flintec.com.

## applications

Load and stress monitoring in structural parts, overload sensing and stability control on mobile and stationary construction and agricultural equipment.

## accessories + options

Compatible on-board load moment indication electronics

Default: Free leads; Optional: M12, 5-pin male code-A connector

RoHS compliant

## key features

Fully welded, stainless steel construction

Hermetically sealed to IP68 for extreme durability

High resolution with low activation force

Bolt-on installation—no adhesives required

Shielded cable for improved signal integrity

Embedded CAN output (user-selectable CANopen or J1939)

Software-configurable parameters for flexible integration

Works with Flintec FDC application for analysis & configuration
