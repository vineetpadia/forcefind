# Single Point Load Cell AK-60 with a Rated Load of 6 ... 60 kg



# Performance Features

- Stainless steel single point load cell
- 3000d OIML R60 and 5000d NTEP approved
- Level of protection: IP68
- Designed for platforms of maximum dimensions 420 mm x 420 mm (off-center load compensated)
- Optimized for dynamic weighing (deflection < 0.25mm)
- Side fixing holes for use in multihead weighers
- ATEX 2014/34/EU version for hazardous area (option)

# Application

- Food industry
- Chemical/pharmaceutical industry
- Dosing equipment in humid and corrosive environments
