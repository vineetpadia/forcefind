# T45 with LA45

Thames Side

## HIGH CAPACITY LOAD CELL ASSEMBLY FOR SILO, TANK AND HOPPER WEIGHING

capacity 50t



Image shows T45 load cell installed within LA45 mounting accessory

The T45 with LA45 Load Cell Assembly is specifically designed as a reliable, simple, low cost solution to your weighing challenges.

The nickel plated alloy steel T45 load cell is mounted within the low profile zinc plated alloy steel LA45 mounting accessory, which has integrated protection against side loads, end loads and lift-off loads. Also included is an allowance for angular misalignment, which is often present in welded metal structures such as silos, tanks or support frames. The wide operating temperature range and protection to IP67 makes the T45 load cell ideal for use in many regions and climates. The durable, tough, shielded 10m Polyurethane cable has high resistance to cuts, impacts, chemical attack and UV radiation. A cable of length 20m is available on request.

Installation is simple and fast, making the T45/LA45 Load Cell Assembly an excellent weighing solution with a very good price/quality ratio.

- Durable nickel plated steel load cell
- Robust zinc plated steel mounting accessory
- Integrated protection against lift-off, side and end loads
- Allows for thermal expansion and contraction of vessel
