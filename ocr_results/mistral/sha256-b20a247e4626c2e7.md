# RC1 compression load cell



## product description

The RC1 is the original compression column load cell designed for applications in truck scales and Process Weighing systems. Constructed from stainless steel and environmentally protected via complete welding the RC1 has been proven to be a robust solution where high capacity and high accuracy is required in industrial weighing applications.

## applications

Truck scales, high capacity scales, silo weighing systems.

## key features

All stainless-steel construction

Hermetically sealed to IP68

Self-restoring column design

Capacities from 250kN to 900kN (25.5t to 91.8t)

Calibration in mV/V/Q

## approvals

OIML approval to C1 (Y = 4,667) and C3 (Y = 10,000)

NTEP approval to 10,000 intervals, Class III L

ATEX hazardous area approval for zones 0, 1, 2, 20, 21 and 22

FM hazardous area approval

## accessories

Compatible range of application hardware

Compatible range of electronics

## wiring

The load cell is provided with a shielded, 4 conductor cable (AWG 20).

Cable jacket: polyurethane

Cable length: 12 m for RC1-250 kN, 16 m for RC1-400 kN, 18 m for RC1-600 and 900 kN

Cable diameter: 7.8 mm

The shield is floating
(On request the shield can be connected to the load cell body)



Specifications and dimensions are subject to change without notice.
