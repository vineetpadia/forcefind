# Standard Internally Gaged Hex Head Cap Screws, SXA Series

Aluminum Alloy

*To measure accurate preload, service load and overload of bolted assemblies*

*For maximum uniformity, efficiency and reliability in bolted assemblies*

*For effective research, testing and manufacturing of bolted assemblies*



U.S. Patent No. 2,873,341

## MECHANICAL SPECIFICATIONS

|  **Material** | Aluminum Alloy 2024-T4  |
| --- | --- |
|  **Hardness** | Rockwell B54 Minimum  |
|  **Ultimate Tensile Strength** | 55,000 psi Minimum; 60,000 psi average  |
|  **Threads** | Class 2A, rolled  |

|  ITEM | CHARACTERISTIC  |

## ALLOWABLE LOADS

Allowable loads for Strainsert Hex Head Cap Screws, as tabulated below, are based on approximately 70% Yield Strength of the threads, in pounds.

|  SCREW SIZE | LOAD LBS.  |
| --- | --- |
|  1/4 – 20NC | 900  |
|  5/16 – 18NC | 1,500  |
|  3/8 – 16NC | 2,200  |
|  1/2 – 13NC | 4,000  |
|  5/8 – 11NC | 6,500  |
|  3/4 – 10NC | 10,000  |

Each cap screw is proof-loaded to above indicated load as part of quality control procedure. Both the value of proof-load and mV/V signal obtained are furnished to customer on a no-charge basis. See calibration section for additional calibration certification.
