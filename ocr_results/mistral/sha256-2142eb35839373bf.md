# L6Q

中航机械

ZEMIC

Measuring Excellence Worldwide



- L6Q load cells are available in the capacities 50kg~250kg.
- Aluminum construction with parallel beam.
- Silicon adhesive sealed, stainless steel cover, surface anodized and anti-corrosion.
- Integral structure and easy installation.
- Suitable for electronic pricing scales, platform scales and other electronic weighing devices.

# Features

- Capacity: 50kg~250kg
- High accuracy
- Max. platform size: 400×400mm
- PTB Approval No: D09-07.06
