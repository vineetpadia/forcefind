# B9C

中软机网 ZEMIC
Measuring Excellence Worldwide







- • B9C load cells are available in the capacities 5Klb~250Klb.
- • Stainless steel construction, potted by adhesive inside, oil proof, waterproof, anti-corrosive gas and medium making it suitable for all kinds of environments.
- • Dual shear beam, suitable for electronic truck scales, automobile testing facilities, track scales, tank scales and other electronic weighing devices.
