# DIGITAL COMPRESSION COLUMN LOAD CELL FOR WEIGHBRIDGES / TRUCK SCALES

capacities 30t, 40t



This digital stainless steel self-centering rocker column load cell is fully welded and hermetically sealed, with ingress protection to IP68 and IP69K standards. It is an ideal solution for weighbridges and truck scales, thanks to its OIML R60 Class C4 approval (4000 divisions) and optional Class C5 approval (5000 divisions). The load cell features built-in lightning surge arrestors and a durable polyurethane cable. Its open protocol allows it to be used with almost any weight indicator. Anti-rotation is ensured by two flat faces on either side of the load cell base, which aligns with corresponding flat faces in the lower mounting cup.

Digital load cells offer several advantages over analogue load cells, including faster and simpler corner adjustments and the ability to diagnose individual load cell faults.

For use in weighbridges/truck scales a set of nickel-plated steel mounting adapters, centering discs and bolts are available as an optional item.

In aggressive environments where stress corrosion or acid attack could be a concern, a special Parylene coating can be specified as an option.

- Open digital protocol
- Fully welded and hermetically sealed to IP68 and IP69K
- In-built surge arrestors for lightning protection
- Simple and fast installation
- Configuration and software can be updated using a serial interface
- Self-centering rocker column design with anti-rotation faces
- Approval to OIML R60 C4 (4000 divisions) optional C5 (5000 divisions)
- Mounting cup accessories available
- Digital interface RS485 (full duplex)
- 3-year warranty

Advantages of the digital T35D load cell over the analogue T35 load cell:

- Higher resolution (up to 1,000,000 steps from zero to full scale)
- Intelligent compensation for each load cell
- Easier, faster maintenance with continuous, individual self-diagnosis
- Direct PC connection via RS485 bus and RS232 converter, up to 1200 metres away
- Internal signal filtering
- Higher protection against EMC interference
- Simpler and faster corner adjustment
- Remote diagnostics via modem using a direct PC connection
