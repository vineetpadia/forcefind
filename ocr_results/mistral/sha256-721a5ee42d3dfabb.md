# ALUMINIUM SINGLE POINT LOAD CELL

capacities 50kg - 1000kg



The T18 single point load cell is manufactured from high quality aluminium alloy. Its design allows for high accuracy weight measurement with off-centre loads. It's potted and sealed to IP67.

Typical applications include: single point platforms up to 800 x 800mm in size, buckets and hoppers supported by a single load cell, bagging & dosing machines, filling scales and checkweighers.

- High quality aluminium alloy load sensor
- Excellent accuracy with off-centre loads
- Potted and sealed to IP67
- Integrated on-centre overload protection (must be set at 150% Emax or less)
- Combined error < ± 0.03%
- 2 Year Warranty
- 800 x 800mm maximum platform size (load cell placed centrally under the platform)
