# 4-CORE SPECIAL LOAD CELL CONNECTION CABLE



This specialist polyurethane 4-core cable is an ideal solution for connecting a junction box to a load cell amplifier/indicator/transmitter.

It provides premium protection of the sensitive internal cores and provides optimum conditions for signal transmission. As well as being extremely tough, the halogen free polyurethane outer sheath has a high resistance to cuts, chemical attack, ultraviolet light, temperature changes and rodents.

- Tinned copper conductors, 7/0.2mm
- 4-core plus shield
- Conductor cross-section 0.22mm²
- Halogen free polyurethane outer sheath
- Service temperature -30°C to +90°C
- Cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE) insulation
