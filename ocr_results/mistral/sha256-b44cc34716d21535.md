# XT960 LOAD CELL AMPLIFIER



The XT960 load cell amplifier is available in surface mount form, with an ABS, Stainless Steel or Die Cast case (all rated to IP65). Operating from AC or DC power and providing 4-20mA and 0-10V analogue outputs as standard, the amplifier can be calibrated in one-pass and set up for use quickly. Options consist of 2 relay set points, RS232 and RS485 communications and a transparent plastic lid to view the LCD display from outside the enclosure. The unit can accommodate both 4-wire and 6-wire load cells.

3 configurable digital inputs are available, for e.g. auto-tare, print, peak hold, reset or other functions.

- Internal 4.5 digit LCD digital display
- Digital programming and calibration
- Simple, one-pass calibration
- 5 volts @ 160 mA excitation, to drive up to 10 x 350 Ohm load cells
- IP65 surface mounting ABS case (Stainless Steel and Die Cast options available)
- Load cell sensitivity range 0.5 to 7.8 mV/V (in 2 ranges configured from keypad)
- Operates on 110-120 VAC, 220-230 VAC or 9-32 VDC
- 20 year data retention for set up values; minimum of 100000 write cycles
