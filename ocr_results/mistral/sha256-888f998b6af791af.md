# Single Point Load Cell AR with a Rated Load of 0.2 ... 1.2 kg



Performance Features

- Aluminum single point load cell
- Very high precision, resolution up to 30.000 divisions
- Protection by varnish or coating
- Designed for platforms of maximum dimensions 150 mm x 150 mm (off-center load compensated)
- Ideally suited for jewelery or laboratory scales

Application

- Jewelry sector
- Chemical /Pharmaceutical Industry
- Laboratory scales
