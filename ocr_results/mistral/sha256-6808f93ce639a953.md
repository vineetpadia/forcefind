### DESCRIPTION

- Stainless steel plate.
- 6-digit backlit LCD display (30 mm height).
- 8-key waterproof keyboard.
- Bubble level and adjustable feet.
- 4 V rechargeable internal battery, 4 Ah capacity.
- Operating time: 70 hours.

### MAIN FUNCTIONS

- Weight indication.
- Piece counting.
- Weight indication as a percentage.
- RS232 output for connection to PC.
