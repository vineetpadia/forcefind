# H6E

中软机械

ZEMIC

Measuring Excellence Worldwide



RoHS

H6E load cells are available in the capacities \(20\mathrm{kg}\sim 300\mathrm{kg}\)
- Alloy steel construction, nickel plated, potted by adhesive inside, oil proof, waterproof, anti-corrosive gas and medium making it suitable for all kinds of environments.
- Single point with parallel beam, suitable for electronic scales, platform scales, belt scales and other electronic weighing devices.
- Recommended platform size: \( {400} \times  {400}\mathrm{\;{mm}} \)
