# HM9B

中航电源

ZEMIC

Measuring Excellence Worldwide



- HM9B load cells are available in the capacities 10t~50t.
- Alloy steel construction, nickel plated, hermetically welded, oil proof, waterproof, anti-corrosive gas and medium making it suitable for all kinds of environments.
- Dual shear beam, suitable for electronic truck scales, railway scales and other electronic weighing devices.

# Features

- Capacity: 10t~50t

- Easy installation and reliable performance
