# SS4000M Series

Miniature Universal Load Cell



## Features

- Available in ranges from ±10N (2.25lb) to ±500N (112 lb)
- Output ±2mV/V nominal
- Accuracy: <± 0.03%/FS
- Miniature, only 35mm (1.38") high
- IP54 Environmental Sealing
- Custom versions available

## Introduction

The U4000M ultra miniature load cell is equally suitable for tension and compression applications and is especially useful where space is limited. The internal construction consist of a full bridge of transducer quality strain gauges that are bonded to the transducer element. This element is manufactured from high strength aluminium alloy or stainless steel, depending upon the force range. The design incorporates side load and bending moment rejection; despite its small size the transducer is highly accurate.



## Benefits

- Improved Accuracy with special designed rod end bearings
- High stiffness and low deflection under load
- Incorporates bending moment and side load compensation
- 100% tested and supplied with an individual calibration certificate
- Male Rod End Bearings available (Option)

## Application

- General in-line force monitoring in limited space applications
- Bi-directional force monitoring
- Tensile testing machine
