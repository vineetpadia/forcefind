# COMPRESSION COLUMN LOAD CELL FOR WEIGHBRIDGES/TRUCK SCALES

capacities 15t - 60t



This stainless steel self-centering rocker column load cell is fully welded and hermetically sealed, with protection to IP68 & IP69K. It's an ideal solution for weighbridges (truck scales) thanks to its approval to OIML C4 (4000 divisions) and integral lightning surge arrestors. As well as being extremely tough, the cable's halogen-free Polyurethane sheath has high resistance to rodents, chemical attack, cuts, ultraviolet light and temperature changes.

Corner adjustment at the junction box is minimised because the T34X has optimized pre-corner adjustment (calibrated in mV/V/Ω by output current matching at ± 0.05%). This saves a significant amount of time during commissioning.

For use in weighbridges (truck scales) a mounting cup accessory set (LA34X) can be ordered separately. Made from nickel plated alloy steel, it consists of a rubber gaiter, a sealing clip and three centering discs.

For applications in aggressive environments where stress corrosion or acid attack could be a problem, a special Parylene coating can be specified as an option. Also available is an ATEX/IECEx version or a High Temperature version, allowing continuous use at up to +150°C.

- Stainless steel load cell
- Fully welded and hermetically sealed to IP68 and IP69K
- In-built surge arrestors for lightning protection
- Industry standard height and mounting pattern
- Calibrated in mV/V/Ω, by output current matching at ± 0.05 %, to minimise corner adjustment
- Simple installation
- Self-centering rocker column design with anti-rotation faces
- Approval to OIML R60 C4 (4000 divisions)
- Wide operating temperature range
- ATEX/IECEx, High Temperature and Parylene coated options available
- 3 Year Warranty
