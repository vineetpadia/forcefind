## Stainless Steel, Welded Seal S-Beam Load Cell

### FEATURES

- Rated capacities of 500 to 20,000 pounds
- Stainless steel, welded seal construction
- Integrated loading brackets
- Compatible with TCM tension mounting hardware
- Designed for single or multiple load cell applications
- Trade certified for NTEP Class IIIL: 10000d and III: 5000d
- Sensorgage™ sealed to IP68 standards
- Factory Mutual System Approved for Classes I, II, III; Divisions 1 and 2; Groups A through G. Also, non-incendive ratings (No barriers!)
- **Optional**
  - Mounting and loading accessory hardware available



### APPLICATIONS

- Hostile environments: Food and beverage processing
  Chemical and plastics processing
  Pharmaceutical and biomedical
- Bin, hopper and belt conveyor scales
- Level and inventory monitoring
- Tension and compression measurements

### DESCRIPTION

The Model 60063 is a stainless steel S-Type load cell. Its welded sealing, combined with high accuracy, make this load cell ideally suited for a wide range of applications of process weighing and force measurement.

Approvals included NTEP III 5000d single and NTEP IIIL10000d multiple. Also available are versions approved for hazardous areas - FM I, II, III Division 1.
