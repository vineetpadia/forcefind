# Rocker Pin Load Cell CB50X with a Rated Load of 5 ... 60 t



Data sheet

Performance Features

- Rocker Pin Load Cell made of stainless steel
- Protection class IP68, welded
- 4000d OIML R60 approved
- Spherical and self centering base
- Anti-rotating system included
- Ideally suited for weighbridge use and silo weighing
- Surge protection (option)
- Anti-rodent shielded cable (option)
- ATEX 2014/34/EU version for hazardous area (option)

Application

- Food industry
- Coal and steel industry
- Chemical / pharmaceutical industry
- Waste management / recycling
- Packaging technology
