# Shear Beam Load Cell SK30X with a Rated Load of 300 kg ... 2 t

Data sheet



# Performance Features

- Shear beam load cell made of stainless steel, hermetically welded with protection class IP68
- Up to 6000d d OIML R60 and 5000d NTEP approved
- Specially designed for low profile industrial platform scales as well as pallet weighing applications
- 6-wire technology
- Calibrated signal output for multi-load cells mounting
- ATEX 2014/34/EU version for hazardous area (option)

# Application

- Hoppers, tanks, mixers or conveyors
- Beverages and food
- Mining, metals and minerals
- Chemistry/Pharmaceuticals
- Waste management/recycling
- Packaging technology
