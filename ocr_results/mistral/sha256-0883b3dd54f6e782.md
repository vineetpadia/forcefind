# DISP-F

# LARGE DIGITAL DISPLAYS

Large digital displays for distance reading.

# Features

DISP-Fx-yz

DISP-F



x = input

- P: process input: 0...10 V and 4(0)...20 mA
- S2: RS-232
- S4: RS-485

y = Digits (4 or 6)

z = Digits height:

- DISP-Fx-y2: 57 mm
- DISP-Fx-y4: 102 mm
- DISP-Fx-y6: 150 mm
- DISP-Fx-y8: 200 mm
- DISP-Fx-y12: 300 mm
- DISP-Fx-y16: 400 mm
- other on request

# Application(s)

The SENSY's DISP-F is perfectly designed for the following applications:

- Load indication on overhead cranes,
- Weighing indication at long distance,
- Indication of measuring force or torque at a distance.

# Function(s)

- Tare by front key or external contact
- Programmation by front key or external contacts
