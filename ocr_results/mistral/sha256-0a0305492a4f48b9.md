# HM9C

中航机械 ZEMIC
Measuring Excellence Worldwide







- HM9C load cells are available in the capacities 5Klb~250Klb.
- Alloy steel construction, hermetically sealed, oil proof, waterproof, anti-corrosive gas and medium making it suitable for all kinds of environments.
- Dual shear beam, suitable for electronic truck scales, automobile testing facilities, track scales and other electronic weighing devices.

# Features

- Capacity 5Klb~250Klb
- High accuracy
- Alloy steel construction
- Easy installation and reliable performance
- PTB Approval No: D09-06.46
