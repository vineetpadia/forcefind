# 2000S

# HERMETICALLY SEALED BENDING BEAM LOAD CELL

Load cell combining high accuracy and resistance in harsh environment.



Model 2000S - 50 kg



# Features

- Material: stainless steel
- Protection class: IP67 (welded sealing)
- Complies with OIML / R 60 up to 3000 d for scales in class III according to EN 45501
- Standardised dimensions
- Cable length: 3 m (other lengths available on request)
- Whole range of mounting kits available

# Most popular options

Application(s) SENSY's load cell 2000S is perfectly designed for the following applications:

- OIML certified and industrial scales,
- Packaging, batching and filling machines,
- Reactors, tanks, vessels or hopper weighing.
