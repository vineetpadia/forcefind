## MOUNTING AND LOAD APPLICATION

The load cells are attached at the mounting holes, the load is applied at the other end. The recommended screws and tightening torques can be found in the table below:

|  Maximum capacities | Thread | Min. property class | Tightening torque^{1)}  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  30...500 kg | M8 | 10.9 | 32 N m  |
|  660 kg | M8 | 12.9 | 39 N m  |

1) Recommended value for the specified property class. Please comply with the screw manufacturer's instructions with regard to screw dimensions

Load must not be applied to the side where the cable connection is located, as this would cause a force shunt.
