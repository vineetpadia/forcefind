## Aluminum Shear Beam Load Cell

FEATURES

- Capacities 300–1000 kg
- Medium profile for industrial platforms
- Anodized aluminum construction
- 4 or 6 Wire (sense) circuit
- IP66 protection
- Low cost



APPLICATIONS

- Low profile platform
- Medical bed weighing
- Tank and silo weighing

DESCRIPTION

Model 3315 is a Medium profile shear beam load cell designed for high accuracy platform scales, pallet scales and process weighing applications.

It has high immunity to shock or side loading and is available in 2 mV/V sensitivity.

A special humidity resistant protective coating assures long-term stability over the entire compensated temperature range.

With 6 wire cable, the two additional sense wires feed back the voltage reaching the load cell. Complete compensation of changes in lead resistance due to temperature change and/or cable extension is achieved by feeding this voltage into the appropriate electronics.
