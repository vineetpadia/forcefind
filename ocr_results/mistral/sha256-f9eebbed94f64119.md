APPROVED DIGITAL CRANE SCALE

LAUMAS®

Capacity up to 12000 kg

DESCRIPTION

- Approved die-cast aluminum crane scale complete with upper shackle and lower hook; suitable for bridge crane.
- 6-digit backlit LCD display (30 mm height).
- IP65 protection rating.
- 7.4 V rechargeable internal battery, 5200 mAh capacity.
- Operating time: 60 hours.
- External battery charger 100-240 VAC included.
- Remote control with range up to 20 m line of sight.

MAIN FUNCTIONS

- Semi-automatic tare (net/gross weight).
- Weight zero setting.
- Tare weight zero setting.
- High resolution visualization.
- Remote control functions: high resolution, zero, net weight, stand-by.
- Energy saving mode.
