# BM14A

中软机械 ZEMIC
Measuring Excellence Worldwide



- BM14A load cells are available in the capacities 10t~100t, 10Klb~500Klb.
- Stainless steel construction, hermetically welded, oil proof, waterproof, anti-corrosive gas and medium making it lightning protected in all kinds of environments.
- Column structure, good self-aligning ability, suitable for electronic platform scales, automobile testing facilities, hopper scales and other electronic weighing devices.

# Features
