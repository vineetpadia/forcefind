# Pressure Sensors

FS01/FS03 Force Sensors

FS Series

FEATURES

- 0 to 1.5 pounds and 0 to 3.0 pound ranges
- High-level output range
- Temperature compensated
- Calibrated zero and span
- Small size
- Low noise

TYPICAL APPLICATIONS

- Medical infusion pumps
- Ambulatory noninvasive pump pressure
- Occulsion detection
- Kidney dialysis machines
- Load and compression sensing
- Variable tensions control



The FS01/FS03 sensors are special low cost, peizoresistive-based force sensors. These high-level voltage output, calibrated, and temperature compensated sensors give an accurate and stable output over a 5 °C to 50 °C [41 °F to 122 °F] temperature range. They offer simple operation from a single 5.0 Vdc supply. Operation from any DC supply voltage, up to 12.0 Vdc, is acceptable.

The FS01/FS03 sensors feature an integrated circuit sensor element and laser trimmed think film ceramic in a small plastic housing. Their extremely small size enables the use of multiple sensors in limited available space. This package also provides excellent corrosion resistance and isolation to external package stress.
