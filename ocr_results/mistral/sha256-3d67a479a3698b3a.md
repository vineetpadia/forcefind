# HIGH ACCURACY SINGLE POINT LOAD CELL WITH INTEGRAL OVERLOAD PROTECTION

capacities 3kg - 100kg



The T6 load cell is specifically designed as a high accuracy, reliable and simple solution for dynamic applications such as multi-head weighers, check weighers and filling machines.

Its integral overload stop offers excellent protection against accidental damage from excessive vertical forces. The aluminium alloy construction, wide operating temperature range and IP66 sealing make it ideally suited for use in a range of different environmental conditions. It exhibits high accuracy with off-centre loads.

- High quality aluminium alloy load sensor
- High accuracy with off-centre loads
- Potted and silicone sealed to IP66
- Wide operating temperature range
- 4000 divisions performance
- Integral overload stop
- 2 year warranty
- 500 x 500mm maximum platform size (load cell placed centrally under the platform)
