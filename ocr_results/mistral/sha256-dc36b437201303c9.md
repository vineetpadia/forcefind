# H2F

中航机械

ZEMIC

Measuring Excellence Worldwide



RoHS

H2F load cells are available for manifold accurate measurement in the capacities 1t-50t.
- Alloy steel, potted with adhesive inside, oil-proof, waterproof and anti-corrosion, suitable for all kinds of environments.
- Spoke type, easy installation, suitable for electronic truck scales, hopper scales and other electronic weighing devices.
- Safe and anti-explosive product, can be used in atrocious environment and hazardous areas.

# Features

- Capacity: 1t~50t
