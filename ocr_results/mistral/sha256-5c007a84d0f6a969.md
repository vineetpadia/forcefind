# TENSION AND COMPRESSION LOAD CELLS (VERY HIGH CAPACITIES)

Accuracy universal load cells, easy to install.



Model 5105



Features

- Wide range of capacities: 10 kN (1 t) up to 50 MN (5.000 t)
- Compact design
- Protection class: IP66
- Material: stainless steel (5100)**, nickel-plated steel (5105)
- Cable length: see drawing table - CL (other lengths available on request)
- Also available as standard reference load cells according to ISO 376 (classes "1", "0,5" and "00" - see specific data sheet 5105-ISO)

Most popular options

Application(s) SENSY's load cells 5100-5105 are perfectly designed for the following applications:

- Industrial force measurement
- Force measurement test bench, hanging load weighing.
