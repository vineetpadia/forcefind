# HM9E

中航机械

ZEMIC

Measuring Excellence Worldwide



RoHS

- HM9E load cells are available in the capacities 50Klb~250Klb,20t~100t.
- Alloy steel construction, hermetically welded, oil proof, waterproof, anti-corrosive gas and medium making it suitable for all kinds of environments.
- Dual shear beam, suitable for electronic truck scales, automobile testing facilities, track scales and other electronic weighing devices.

# Features

- Capacity 5Klb~250Klb; 20t~100t

- Easy installation and reliable performance
