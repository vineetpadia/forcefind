# SB61C single point load cell



## product description

The SB61C is an economical alternative to the Flintec SB8 beam load cell. The SB61C is designed to be a drop-in replacement for the SB8 for applications that do not require trade approved weighing performance. Constructed from aluminium and environmentally sealed with potting compound to ensure durability. Available in a single capacity of 50kg.

## applications

Process Weighing systems, bagging machinery.

## accessories

Compatible range of electronics

## key features

Aluminium construction

Low profile design

Environmentally sealed by potting to IP67

Bolt-hole compatible with SB6, SB8 and ZLB

For platform sizes of up to 250 x 250mm

Rated output with tight tolerances for easy load cell replacement, no recalibration required

Capacity of 50kg

RoHS compliant
