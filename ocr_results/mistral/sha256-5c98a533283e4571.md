# PC6H single point load cell



## product description

The PC6H is designed for rear-end bin lifting systems on waste collection vehicles. Certified to both OIML and NTEP standards the PC6H is the ideal choice for trade approved waste collection applications. The fully welded, stainless steel design and rugged construction overcomes the problems associated with alternative aluminium load cell based systems.

Alternative load cell sizes and bolt-hole configurations are available in the Flintec bin-lift load cell range – see our PC2H, PC3H, PC5H, and PC7H single point load cells.

## applications

Rear end (REL) bin lifting systems for waste collection vehicles (RCV's)

## options

Variable cable lengths

Can be supplied with connectors

## accessories

Compatible range of electronics

RoHS compliant

## key features

Capacity of 2,000kg

Stainless steel construction with a bead-blasted surface

Hermetically sealed to IP68/IP69K

Very rugged construction

Off-centre load adjusted

High accuracy

For platform sizes of up to 1,200mm x 1,200mm

## approvals

OIML approval to C3 (Y=21,900)

NTEP approved to 3,000 intervals, Class III, single cell applications
