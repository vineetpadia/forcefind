# Force sensor Nesthorn

## Datasheet KZ-011-0400

Force is applied through the center

### Benefits & characteristics

- Measurement ranges: 0 – 18 N to 0 – 300 N
- Force is applied to a round-head rivet
- Compact and robust design
- Easy mounting: The sensor can be mounted in a blind hole with a diameter of 12 mm
- Available in several force ranges and variants
- Optional stainless-steel housing
