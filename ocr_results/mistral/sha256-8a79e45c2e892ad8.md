# ALUMINIUM SINGLE POINT LOAD CELL

capacities 0.3kg - 3kg



This double bending beam single point load cell is manufactured from high quality aluminium alloy. Its design allows for high accuracy weight measurement with off-centre loads. It's potted and sealed to IP66.

Typical applications include single point platforms up to 200 x 200mm in size, dosing machines as well as filling scales.

- High quality aluminium alloy load sensor
- High accuracy with off-centre loads
- Potted and silicone sealed to IP66
- Wide operating temperature range
- 3000 divisions performance
- 2 year warranty
- 200 x 200mm maximum platform size (load cell placed centrally under the platform)
