# Single Point Load Cell AP with a Rated Load of 75 ... 635 kg





# Performance Features

- Aluminum single point load cell
- 3000d OIML R60 and 5000d NETP approved
- Level of protection: IP65
- Designed for platforms of maximum dimensions 700 mm x 700 mm (off-center load compensated)
- Super flat construction
- ATEX 2014/34/EU version for hazardous area (option)

# Application

- Food industry
- Coal and steel industry
- Chemical/pharmaceutical industry
- Waste management/recycling
- Packaging technology
