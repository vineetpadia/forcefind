# LOW-PROFILE (PANCAKE) LOAD CELLS

Universal tension and compression load cells for industrial force measurement.



Model 2960 - 300 kN



Features

- Flat design
- High stiffness and fatigue resistant
- Highly resistant to transverse forces
- Protection class: IP66
- Complete range of mounting accessories available
- Material: stainless steel (2960)**, anodised aluminium alloy (2962), alloy steel (2965)
- Cable length: see drawing table - CL (other lengths available on request)

Most popular options

Coat Wholesome bridge

SIL / PL ready

Amplified output

Digital output

USB

Wireless

60°C...+160°C

Application(s) SENSY's load cells 2960-2962-2965 are perfectly designed for the following applications:

- Force measurement on machines, industrial process control, hydraulic cylinders monitoring, fatigue tests,
- Hydraulic cylinders monitoring.
