## Shear Beam Load Cell

### FEATURES

- Capacities 500–10000 kg.
- EDOC coated Steel construction
- IP67/IP69K protection
- **Optional**
  - EEx ia IIC T6 hazardous area approval
  - FM and IECEx approvals available

### APPLICATIONS

- Low profile platforms
- Pallet truck weighing
- Tank and silo weighing

### DESCRIPTION

The Model 3410ES is a low profile shear beam load cell designed for high accuracy platform scales, pallet scales and process weighing applications.

It has high immunity to shock or side loading and is available in 2 mV/V sensitivity. For Hazardous environments this load cell is available with EEx ia IIC T6 level of European approval.





Enhanced Environmental protection through EDOC coating and bonded recessed covers for long-term reliability under harsh environment.
