# APPLICATION EXAMPLE: HOW TO MAKE MOUNTING KITS WITH HORIZONTAL CONSTRAINTS

By a copper wire, connect the upper supporting plate with the lower supporting plate, then link together the lower plates to the earthing system.



To ensure the stability of the structure, the system designer must predict any further precaution against side shifts and anti-tilt in function of: knocks and vibrations, wind effect, seismic conditions and hardness of support structure.
