# Typical Applications

» Platform scales (max. 350mm x 350mm) » Filling scales
» Bagging machines » Dosing scales

The T2 double bending beam, single point load cell is ideal for high-accuracy weight measurement with off-centre loads.

It is manufactured from high-quality aluminium alloy and silicone sealed to IP66. It offers 3000 divisions accuracy.

Static overload of 150% (with central loads) is permitted without affecting weighing performance.

The low profile (low height) design allows for excellent weighing performance. This reduces the cost of the scale design, giving an economical product without affecting functionality.

Platform scales up to 350mm x 350mm can be supported by a single model T2 load cell, which weighs within its tolerance limits regardless of the load application point thanks to its off-centre load compensation.
