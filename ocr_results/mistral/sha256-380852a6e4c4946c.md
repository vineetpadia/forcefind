# THROUGH HOLE (ANNULAR) LOAD CELLS

Annular force transducers specially designed for applications requiring a load measurement through a centred hole.



Model 5900 - 20 t



Features

- o Sturdy design
- o Material: stainless steel
- o Protection class: IP67
- o Easy to install
- o CE certified for hoisting applications
- o Complete range of "CE" certified electronics and load limiters
- o Cable length: 6 m (other lengths available on request)

Most popular options

Application(s) SENSY's load cells 5900 are perfectly designed for the following applications:

Load limitation on EOT cranes, industrial weighing, force measurement,...
