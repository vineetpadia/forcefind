### TQ202/TQM202 Series



Standard

- ✓ All Stainless Steel Case for Long-Term Reliability in Industrial Environments
- ✓ High Accuracy
- ✓ Shielded Cable for Precise Low-Noise Measurements
- ✓ Heavy-Duty Mounting for Positive, Non-Slip Connection

OMEGA's TQ202/TQM202 Series torque sensors are small, high-accuracy flange- or surface-mount devices designed for measuring reaction torque forces up to 250 inch-lb. The shaft attachment has 3 equally spaced flat surfaces to ensure non-slip mounting without the use of a key. The all stainless steel construction ensures long life in industrial environments, as well as long-term accuracy.
