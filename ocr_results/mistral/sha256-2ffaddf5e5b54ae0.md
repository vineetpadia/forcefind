# Single Point Load Cell AL with a Rated Load of 3 ... 30 kg



# Performance Features

- Aluminum single point load cell
- 3000d OIML R60 and 5000d NTP approved
- Level of protection: IP65
- Designed for platforms of maximum dimensions 300 mm x 350 mm (off-center load compensated)
- Low cost model for high volume production
- ATEX 2014/34/EU version for hazardous area (option)

# Application

- Food industry
- Chemical/pharmaceutical industry
- Dosing equipment in humid and corrosive environments
