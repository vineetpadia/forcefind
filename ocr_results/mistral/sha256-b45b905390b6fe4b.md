# Single-Point Stainless Steel Load Cell

FEATURES

- Capacity range: 10–100 kg
- Stainless steel construction
- Single-point 350 × 350 mm platform
- IP66 protection

APPLICATIONS

- Retail scales
- Counting scales
- Bench scales
- Harsh environments

DESCRIPTION

The Model 93006 is a low profile single-point load cell designed for direct mounting in retail, bench, and counting scales and a wide range of other scale applications.



Its small physical size combined with high accuracy and low cost makes this load cell ideally suited for new scale construction.

This load cell's stainless steel construction makes it ideal for use in corrosive and wet environments that are not appropriate for common aluminum load cells.
