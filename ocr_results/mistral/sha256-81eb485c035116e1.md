# Tension and Compression Load Cell ZA30X with a Rated Load of 500 kg ... 7.5 t

Data sheet



Performance Features

- Tension and Compression load cell made of stainless steel, hermetically welded with protection class IP68
- 3000d OIML R60 approved
- Ideally suited for hanging tension- or compression load cell and force measurement
- Calibrated signal output for multi-load cells mounting
- ATEX 2014/34/EU version for hazardous area (option)

Application

- Food industry
- Coal and steel industry
- Chemical / pharmaceutical industry
- Waste management / recycling
- Packaging technology
