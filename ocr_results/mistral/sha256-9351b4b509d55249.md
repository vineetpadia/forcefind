## Double-Ended Shear Beam Load Cell

**FEATURES**

- Capacities 40 klb. & 50 klb.
- Coated Alloy Steel construction (Stainless Steel Available)
- Trade certified for NTEP class IIIL, 10000 divisions.
- IP67 protection

**OPTIONAL**

FM Approved versions for use in potentially explosive atmospheres

**APPLICATIONS**

- Truck scales
- Railroad track scales
- Precision tank, bin, and silo weighing.

**DESCRIPTION**

The Model 65061 is a mid to high capacity, Zinc or Nickel coated Alloy Steel, double ended shear beam load cell.

This product is designed for use in certified truck and railScales and is available in capacities 40 klb. and 50 klb.



This load cell is optionally rated intrinsically safe by the Factory Mutual System (FM Approved for USA and Canada) making it suitable for use in potentially explosive environments. This load cell is certified for legal for trade applications by NIST to NTEP class IIIL requirement.
